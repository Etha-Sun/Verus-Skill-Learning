# Single-problem token-cost evolution figure caption

Token-cost screening and matched confirmation for the intentionally overfit
IronKV task `delegation_map_v__impl4__range_consistent_impl`. Panel (a) shows
one Codex solver trajectory per skill for the three Token R6 seeds and the
three skills produced in each of meta-evolution rounds R1-R3. Bars report
Expected Primary Uncached Tokens to Success (ETtS); for a valid one-attempt,
one-success condition this equals that run's primary uncached tokens.
Percentages are relative to the fresh H0 mean. The shaded band is the observed
H0 range (82.8k-95.7k), the dashed line is the H0 mean (87.3k), and the
hatched R3 bar is the candidate selected for confirmation. The Token R6
structural seed timed out without terminal usage and is marked invalid rather
than assigned a token value.

Panel (b) shows all three fresh H0 trajectories and all three fresh
`local-proof-surface-cap` confirmation trajectories. Bar heights are the
condition ETtS/means and vertical lines span the observed run ranges. Both
conditions are 3/3 verifier-safe with complete F3 and terminal-usage evidence.
The final skill's ETtS is 82.4k, 5.64% below H0, but the 4.9k favorable mean
difference is smaller than H0's own 12.9k range. The predeclared conclusion is
therefore inconclusive within H0 variation, not a stable token-efficiency
improvement.
